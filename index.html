About us

Nova Rocketry provides students with hands-on engineering experience, with a particular focus on the aerospace industry. Members gain practical experience in rocket design, manufacturing, programming, and launch operations, developing skills that extend beyond the classroom and help prepare them for their future careers in the industry.
